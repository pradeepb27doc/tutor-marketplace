# Tutor Marketplace PRD

## 1. Product Summary

Build India's most trusted education services marketplace for parents to discover, compare, book, pay for, and manage verified tutors and activity coaches for children aged 3 to 15.

The product should feel closer to Urban Company or Uber than a traditional classifieds directory: high trust, fast matching, clear prices, reliable scheduling, secure payments, strong quality controls, and measurable student outcomes.

## 2. Vision

Create the default platform Indian parents use when they need a reliable private tutor, skills coach, or enrichment class for a child.

Long term, the platform becomes a full education services marketplace spanning home tuition, online tuition, group classes, weekend classes, holiday camps, trial classes, recorded learning products, institute SaaS, and API-driven education infrastructure.

## 3. Strategic Principles

- Parent trust comes before marketplace growth.
- Booking must be possible in under 2 minutes for high-intent parents.
- Student progress must be visible, not assumed.
- Tutor supply quality matters more than tutor quantity.
- Every major workflow should improve Weekly Completed Classes.
- The marketplace must be designed for repeat usage, not one-off lead generation.
- Safety, payments, messaging, and identity must be platform-owned.
- The MVP must launch narrowly enough to earn trust quickly.

## 4. North Star Metric

Weekly Completed Classes.

This is the best early signal that parents are finding value, tutors are receiving demand, scheduling is working, payments are flowing, and students are actively learning.

## 6. Target Users

### Parents

Parents are the primary paying customer. They need quick discovery, strong verification, clear pricing, transparent reviews, child-safe workflows, convenient scheduling, reliable communication, and proof that the child is improving.

### Students

Students are children aged 3 to 15. Their experience should be simple, age-appropriate, encouraging, and focused on upcoming classes, homework, learning streaks, practice, and progress visibility.

### Tutors and Providers

Service providers include private tutors, tuition centers, coaching institutes, music teachers, dance teachers, coding mentors, language teachers, art teachers, chess coaches, robotics trainers, public speaking coaches, and sports coaches.

They need a predictable way to receive bookings, manage calendars, set prices, communicate with families, track students, receive payments, and grow their reputation.

### Admin and Operations

Admin users include super admins, verification teams, support teams, finance operators, trust and safety reviewers, and growth operators.

They need workflows for verification, quality control, dispute resolution, refunds, fraud monitoring, content management, analytics, coupons, and notifications.

## 7. Market Positioning

The platform is not a listing site, school ERP, learning management system, or generic video classroom.

It is a managed marketplace for verified education services where the platform owns trust, transaction flow, scheduling, payments, communication, quality measurement, and repeat usage.

## 8. Product Scope

### MVP Scope

The first shippable product should support one city or region, a constrained set of subjects, verified individual tutors, parent-led booking, secure payments, and core admin operations.

MVP should include:

- Parent registration and login
- Parent profile
- Multiple child profiles
- Tutor discovery
- Tutor profiles
- Subject and grade filters
- Online and home tuition modes
- Trial class booking
- Regular class booking request
- Tutor availability slots
- Booking acceptance or rejection
- Payment capture for confirmed bookings
- Basic cancellation and refund rules
- Tutor onboarding
- Tutor KYC status tracking
- Tutor subject and pricing setup
- Tutor calendar management
- Ratings and reviews
- Attendance marking
- Basic homework tracking
- Parent notifications
- Tutor notifications
- Admin tutor verification
- Admin booking management
- Admin payment overview
- Audit logs for sensitive actions

### Post-MVP Scope

Post-MVP should add recurring subscriptions, richer student dashboards, real-time chat, video consultations, AI tutor matching, AI study plans, AI progress reports, wallet withdrawals, referral programs, coupons, institute accounts, group classes, camps, dynamic pricing, waitlists, support tickets, fraud detection, and multi-city expansion.

### Out of Scope for MVP

- Mock test marketplace
- API platform
- Fully automated dynamic pricing
- Fully automated AI homework grading
- Complex wallet settlement automation
- Multi-country payments
- Public marketplace advertisements
- Advanced offline support beyond graceful mobile recovery

## 9. Core User Journeys

### Parent Booking Journey

1. Parent opens the app.
2. Parent selects or adds a child.
3. Parent searches for a subject or goal.
4. Platform recommends tutors based on location, grade, mode, availability, price, rating, and verification.
5. Parent applies filters.
6. Parent views tutor profile.
7. Parent chooses trial or regular class.
8. Parent selects date, time, mode, and child.
9. Parent pays securely or authorizes payment.
10. Tutor accepts or rejects the booking.
11. Notifications are sent to both sides.
12. Class is completed.
13. Attendance is marked.
14. Parent rates the class.
15. Platform encourages repeat booking or package conversion.

### Tutor Onboarding Journey

1. Tutor registers with mobile OTP and email.
2. Tutor completes profile details.
3. Tutor submits government ID.
4. Tutor submits degree or qualification proof.
5. Tutor submits experience proof where available.
6. Tutor selects subjects, grades, service modes, location coverage, pricing, and availability.
7. Admin verifies the tutor.
8. Tutor becomes searchable after approval.
9. Tutor receives booking requests.
10. Tutor accepts bookings and completes classes.
11. Tutor receives earnings in wallet after settlement rules pass.

### Student Learning Journey

1. Student sees upcoming classes.
2. Student attends class.
3. Tutor marks attendance.
4. Tutor assigns homework or practice.
5. Student submits homework.
6. Parent sees class summary and progress.
7. Platform tracks streaks, completed classes, weak areas, and goals.

### Admin Verification Journey

1. Admin views pending tutor applications.
2. Admin reviews identity, education, experience, and profile quality.
3. Admin approves, rejects, or requests changes.
4. Every decision is audit logged.
5. Verified tutors enter the marketplace supply pool.

## 15. AI Requirements

AI features should be introduced only when the underlying product data is reliable.

MVP AI readiness:

- Capture structured tutor attributes.
- Capture child profile and learning goals.
- Capture booking, attendance, homework, rating, and progress events.
- Store clean analytics events for recommendation quality.

Initial AI module candidates:

- Tutor matching assistant
- Parent search intent parser
- Study plan generator
- Progress summary generator
- Homework helper
- Practice question generator

Later AI module candidates:

- Weakness detection
- Personalized learning paths
- Revision planner
- Tutor performance analytics
- Fraud detection

AI safety requirements:

- Do not provide medical, legal, or high-stakes child safety decisions automatically.
- Do not allow unreviewed AI-generated claims on tutor profiles.
- Clearly separate verified facts from generated recommendations.
- Log AI recommendation inputs and outputs for auditability.

## 17. Non-Functional Requirements

Performance:

- API p95 response time under 200 ms for common read endpoints.
- Mobile app startup under 2 seconds on target devices.
- Search results should appear quickly and support pagination.

Security:

- JWT-based authentication.
- OTP login support.
- Role-based access control.
- Input validation on every API boundary.
- Rate limiting for authentication, search, booking, and messaging.
- Secure storage for documents and uploaded media.
- Audit logging for admin and financial actions.

Reliability:

- Idempotent payment and booking operations.
- Background jobs for notifications, settlement, analytics, and reminders.
- Retry policies for external service failures.
- Observability through Sentry and structured logs.

Accessibility:

- Mobile-first UI.
- Sufficient contrast in light and dark mode.
- Clear touch targets.
- Screen-reader-friendly labels.
- Avoid using color alone to indicate state.

Localization:

- English-first MVP.
- Architecture must support Indian language localization.
- Priority languages after MVP: Hindi and Kannada.

Scalability:

- Clean Architecture and Domain-Driven Design.
- Repository pattern for data access.
- Dependency injection.
- Event-driven workflows for booking, payment, notifications, and analytics.
- Redis caching for hot reads.
- BullMQ background workers.
- Feature flags for controlled rollout.

## 19. MVP Release Criteria

The MVP can launch when:

- Parents can register, add children, search tutors, view profiles, book, pay, and receive notifications.
- Tutors can onboard, submit verification details, configure profile, manage availability, accept bookings, and mark class completion.
- Admins can verify tutors, manage bookings, review payments, and inspect audit logs.
- A completed class can move through the full lifecycle from search to payment to attendance to review.
- Payment callbacks are idempotent and tested.
- Booking state transitions are validated and tested.
- Role-based access control is enforced.
- Critical flows have unit and integration tests.
- Seed data exists for demos and QA.
- Basic documentation exists for setup, architecture, API usage, and operations.

## 20. Risks and Mitigations

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Low-quality tutor supply | Parent trust loss | Strict verification, probation period, rating thresholds, manual curation |
| Booking cancellations | Marketplace reliability loss | Availability discipline, cancellation penalties, substitute recommendations |
| Payment disputes | Financial and trust risk | Clear policies, escrow-style flow, audit logs, support tooling |
| Child safety concerns | Existential platform risk | KYC, check-in/out OTP, reporting, moderated communication, admin review |
| Overbuilding before liquidity | Slow launch | City-limited, category-limited MVP with manual ops support |
| AI hallucinations | Trust and safety risk | AI as assistive layer, source-backed outputs, audit logs, human review |
| Tutor churn | Supply instability | Fair earnings, clear payouts, performance insights, predictable demand |
| Parent churn | Revenue loss | Progress visibility, reminders, packages, quality control |
